# TDDE78

The [Course Website](https://www.ida.liu.se/~TDDE78/)

## What I Learned in This Course
- Reinforcement learning fundamentals: Markov decision processes, value functions, policies and the exploration-exploitation trade-off
- Value-based deep RL: DQN, Double DQN and Dueling DQN with experience replay and target networks
- Policy gradient methods: REINFORCE with baselines and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) with GAE
- Actor-critic methods: A2C with Generalized Advantage Estimation and Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) for continuous control
- Model-based deep RL: neural world models, Dyna-style planning and Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)
- Multi-agent deep RL: MAPPO and MADDPG under the centralized training, decentralized execution (CTDE) paradigm
- Practical deep RL engineering: PyTorch, Gymnasium and PettingZoo environments, ablation studies and evaluation across multiple seeds
